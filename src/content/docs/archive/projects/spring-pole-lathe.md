## Spring Pole Lathe

<img src="/dms-source/files/mw/a_a4_SpringPole0.jpg" width="620" height="411" /> Spring Pole Lathe Class From Roy Underhill's School


The design for this lathe is inspired by an old German design published by [Roy Underhill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Underhill) in episode 5 of season 24 of the [Woodwright's Shop](http://www.pbs.org/woodwrightsshop/). While I am following his published design closely, I am going to use some modern machining to make the conical points run much more smoothly and with considerably less friction.
