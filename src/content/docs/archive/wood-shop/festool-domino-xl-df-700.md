The Domino XL is a loose mortise and tenon joining tool manufactured by the German company Festool. Dominoes (loose tenons) are manufactured by Festool. A small selection is made available in the Woodshop, please pay for Dominoes used -- they're expensive.

The OEM manual can be found here: <http://www.festoolusa.com/media/pdf/Domino_XL_DF700.pdf>
