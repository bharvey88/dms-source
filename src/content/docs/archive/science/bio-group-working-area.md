The previous group established themselves during the [launched the Makerspace Bio Program](/dms-source/archive/completed-events/2011-open-house-bio-launch/) in our new lab at the Dallas Makerspace 2011 Open House.

Around mid 2012 the last bio committee disbanded and their equipment was divided up/sold. It was restarted in mid 2013 by Andrew Floyd with a new direction and lab area.

What's going on now?

- The lab is up and running in the new Makerspace!
- We are making a series of [Bio lab instructional videos](/dms-source/archive/science/bio-lab-instructional-videos/) to explain how to use each piece of equipment
- We are brewing beer, making mead, and experimenting with vegetable fermentation.
- We are meeting weekly after the Thursday 7pm member's meeting (usually around 8:30pm)
- We still need equipment, see this [list of lab equipment](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/List_of_lab_equipment) (but it is out of date and changing)
- List of [people interested in bio projects](/dms-source/archive/science/people-interested-in-bio-projects/) that make up the Bio Projects Group

Also see the page of [meeting notes for the bio projects group](/dms-source/archive/science/meeting-notes-for-the-bio-projects-group/)
