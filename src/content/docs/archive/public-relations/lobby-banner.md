## **Welcome!**

- Everyone *MUST* sign a waiver using the nearby tablet kiosks, including one-time visitors & prospective members. Parent/guardian must sign for persons aged 17 & under.
- Masks must be worn at all times.
- Social distancing must be observed at all times.
- Only Dallas Makerspace members can use our tools.
- Persons aged 15 & under MUST be accompanied by a parent/guardian at ALL times.
- Be EXCELLENT to our all-volunteer workforce by leaving the 'Space cleaner than you found it.
- Donations of tools/supplies MUST be pre-approved by a committee chair (one’s trash is NOT always another's treasure)
