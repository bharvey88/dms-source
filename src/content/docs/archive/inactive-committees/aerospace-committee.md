## About

The Aerospace / RC Committee covers:

- UAVs/Drones
- Radio Controlled Aircraft
- Radio Controlled Vehicles
- Rocketry
- [High Altitude Ballooning](/dms-source/archive/aerospace/dms-high-altitude-balloon/)

## Purpose

1.  Basic and advanced education about radio controlled aircraft, vehicles, and rockets
2.  Work on individual and group projects
3.  Promote interest in the radio control and rocketry hobbies
