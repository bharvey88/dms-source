This is a stub for a committee that has been [proposed on Talk](https://talk.dallasmakerspace.org/t/bod-agenda-7-24-19-establishment-of-volunteer-recognition-comittee/59140/). The sponsors of this should consider defining the role of this proposed committee.

## About

The Volunteer Recognition Committee covers:

- Positive reinforcement of volunteerism around DMS
- Recruitment of new volunteers
- Recognition initiatives for members going above and beyond for the betterment of DMS
- Tracking and coordination of volunteers as requested

## Purpose

1.  To build a stronger sense of community among the growing member base of DMS
2.  Ensure that volunteers feel appreciated and seen
3.  Encourage and engage members to volunteer for the betterment of DMS
