The KMG-10 is a 10" belt grinder with various attachments/platens for several kinds of grinding work on various types of stock.

## Manufacturer's Description

- 10″ contact wheel.
- Platen attachment for flat grinding.
- Work rest.
- Tool arm.
- KMG Chassis.
