An account on dallasmakerspace.org gives you access to the following:

- Editing the Dallas Makerspace Wiki: www.dallasmakerspace.org/wiki
- Create Blog Articles: <http://www.dallasmakerspace.org/blog/admin>
- Access the Forum: <https://dallasmakerspace.org/forums/>
- Access your Billing Account: <https://dallasmakerspace.org/accounts/clientarea.php>

## How To Get An Account

An account for dallasmakerspace.org is created when you become a member ([Link](https://dallasmakerspace.org/accounts/cart.php?gid=1)).
