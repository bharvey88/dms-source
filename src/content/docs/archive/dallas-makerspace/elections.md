## Elections

This page describes requirements and procedures for Board of Director elections. Committee elections are not included in this information.

### Requirements

The [DMS Bylaws](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/bylaws/#Section_4.6.1_Board_Election_Meeting) require an election of the Board of Directors at least once per calendar year. Detailed requirements for the Board election are also specified in the [Bylaws](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/bylaws/#Section_4.6.1_Board_Election_Meeting).

### Procedures

The [current election procedure is described at this link](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/board-of-directors-annual-election-procedures/).

### Voting Rights

- The policy regarding member voting rights is presented in the [Rules & Policies](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/rules-and-policies/#Voting_Rights).
- Voting rights must be requested; they are not granted automatically. Clarification is provided in the [New Member 411](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/new-member-411/#Voting), including how to request voting rights.
