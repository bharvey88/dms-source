### Overview

This page documents the definition of terms and rules around the legal concepts of "Fijudiciary", "Disqualified Parties" and "Self Dealing" within the context of Dallas Makerspace.

As the Dallas Makerspace grows, so does our legal accountability as a non-profit corporation. We as an organization need to be aware of our legal bounds with spending and purchases.

While it might seem unfair or misleading, there are laws that maintain a higher standard than basic and pure honesty and integrity. We as a non-profit corporation need to ensure that we also keep the appearance of impropriety at bay.

This page is broken into 4 groups; terms and definitions, rules around those terms, use cases of types of activities at Dallas Makerspace, how we as Dallas Makerspace apply these rules internally.
