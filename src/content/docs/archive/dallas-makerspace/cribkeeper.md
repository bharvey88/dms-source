The Cribkeeper is in charge of tracking Makerspace property that has been loaned out. No items may be removed from the space without being cleared by the Cribkeeper.

The current Cribkeeper is: Lance Preston.

## Items Available For Loan

Only items listed below can be loaned out. Each committee is responsible for the items in their room, and must designate an item as available for loan before it can be placed on this list:

### Darkroom/Photography Lab

- Cameras

### Automotive

- Brake bleeder
- Pressure washer
