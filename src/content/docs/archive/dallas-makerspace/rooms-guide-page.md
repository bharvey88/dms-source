## Classrooms Guide

There are five general usage "classrooms" available for members to schedule at Dallas Makerspace. Most have some type of electronic projection capability, documented at this link on the [Classrooms page](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/classroom/). Select the room that is most appropriate for your class.

*Per the fire code, the max capacity for each of our classrooms is 49 persons (no classroom has more than one usable exit).*

Rules regarding classes and external events are listed [at this link](https://source.dallasmakerspace.org/display/RULES/Rules+and+Policies#RulesandPolicies-Classes&Events).
