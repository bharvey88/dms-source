## Statement of Intent

This letter is to serve as a formal acceptance of nomination for a role on the Dallas Makerspace Board of Directors. I, Nicole Greeley, if elected, will uphold the bylaws put forth and voted upon by members of the DMS. This would include, but is not limited to, upholding the voted upon DMS Standing Rules and policies as well as all preforming the duties of a director as stated in the bylaws. I will, to the best of my ability, keep an open mind and ear to all issues that arise within the organization. I will strive to expand awareness of the orginization and put in time and effort to see that the space is maintained. I would love to see this group thrive and expand and look forward to working for the betterment of DMS and its members.

## Conflict of Interests

I have no known conflicts of interests that I am aware of at this time.
