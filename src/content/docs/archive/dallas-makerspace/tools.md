This is a (non-comprehensive) list of tools at the Dallas Makerspace. This list will help us keep track of information about the tools, their general status (through the **[Area Cameras Page](https://dallasmakerspace.org/cam/)** and the **[DMS Tool Status Board](https://dallasmakerspace.org/cgi-bin/toolstatus.cgi)** which is often out of date), and who donated/loaned it. Per the Dallas Makerspace [Code of Conduct](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/rules-and-policies/#Code_of_Conduct), **all tools/resources must stay on the premises** so that other members may use them.

Many of the specialized tools at Dallas Makerspace are dangerous or easily damaged if improperly used. If you are unsure how to safely operate a piece of equipment, find someone who knows how to use the equipment and ask for help.

If you are looking for a training class, keep an eye on the [Dallas Makerspace Talk Forum](https://talk.dallasmakerspace.org/) and the [DMS Events Calendar](http://dallasmakerspace.org/calendar/). If no class is scheduled, instigate one by asking the mailing list for any other potential students and members willing to teach the class.

**TOOL PERMISSIONS:** If a tool in the list below is marked as requiring a class, **you must complete a class and/or get approval from the committee responsible for that equipment**. **You can see your list of approved tools when you sign into your "Who Am I" DMS account [at this link](http://whoami.dallasmakerspace.org/).** Each approved tool is listed as an "Assigned Group". (If it gives you an error message, you need to log in).


**FEES:** Check out the **[DMS_Additional_Fees](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/dms-additional-fees/) page** for ancillary fees and how to make those payments. You can use [this online link](/dms-source/archive/dallas-makerspace/fund-qr-codes/) to pay any fees.
