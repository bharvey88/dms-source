## Time to Make Plans for the New 3D Fab Room

As the lease at our current location comes to an end on July 1st, we must begin making plans for our New 3D Fab Room at the New Dallas Makerspace Location. The 3D Fabrication Room will be located in room \#04 at the new 'Space in Suite \#104 at: 1825 Monetary Ln \#104 Carrollton, TX 75006



## Let's Build a Custom Filament Cabinet

The Board of Directors has approved spending so we can purchase or build storage for the 3D Printer Filament. I would like to look into building two or three of these - optimally one near each 3D Printer Table.

Preferred: Six Lines (can be three drops x two lines)\

1x: For the Mine Craft Server  ;)\

## Misc Requests

I would like to request that the three recliner chairs currently located in the Ladybird Lounge be used in our New 3D Fab Room - **IF** they aren't going to be used in a lounge-type area at the new 'Space. They will fit nicely and will provide a place for members to comfortably wait in the 3D Fab Room while their files print.

- <img src="/dms-source/files/mw/thumb_7_7a_20140509_052617.jpg_120px-20140509_052617.jpg" width="120" height="68" alt="20140509 052617.jpg" />
- <img src="/dms-source/files/mw/thumb_2_20_20140509_052142.jpg_120px-20140509_052142.jpg" width="120" height="68" alt="20140509 052142.jpg" />


I would like to request that the "Element" Flat Screen TV mounted on the wall in the current Ladybird Lounge be used in our New 3D Fab Room - **IF** it isn't going to be used in a better suited area at the new 'Space. This would serve the following purposes:

- Members will be able to 'cast' instructional YouTube videos to learn more about the 3D Fabrication Processes available.
- Members will be able to view miscellaneous programming at times no-one else is watching educational material on the TV.
- At times that members are not actively watching content via Chromecast, a playlist of 3D Fabrication Related Instructional Videos can be played from the local file server.
