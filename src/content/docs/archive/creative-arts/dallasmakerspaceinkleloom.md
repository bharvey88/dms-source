## Classroom mini-Inkle Loom

This miniature inkle loom was designed for use in teaching basic weaving techniques at the dallas makerspace. The design is simple and easy to replicate with either modern CNC equipment or with simple hand tools. This allows for students to obtain personal looms to continue to learn and experiment as their interest dictates.

### The Design

The design file is a basic Inkscape drawing. It is designed to be cut from 3/4" (nominal) wood. Furniture Grade plywood works, as well as most hardwoods.
