## August 2013

Embedded Workshop will show how to control small motors or other loads using an Arduino. You need to bring

- your Arduino
- breadboard
- 12V power supply for the motor. If your motor or load is 12V, you can bring it. We have ten 12V motors plus NPN

transistors, resistors, and hookup wire to complete the basic circuit.

The class starts at 7PM Aug 7th in the Ladybird classroom at DMS.
