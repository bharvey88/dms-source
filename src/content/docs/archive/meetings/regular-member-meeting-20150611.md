- [Infrastructure Committee](/dms-source/archive/infrastructure/) - Andrew LeCody
  - A few HVAC units servicing the Warehouse areas were leaking onto ceiling tiles. The ceiling tiles were removed due to damage and will be replaced.
    - The cause of this turned out to be a clogged drain line that is shared with all of our suite's HVAC units. A plumber was called and is working on clearing the lines.
    - In the meantime, the drain lines were disconnected (so the condensation "drains" on the roof itself) to avoid damage to the ceiling tiles and and tools near the affected areas.
  - Two network drops were flipped from the back of the Lecture Hall to the new Arts room.
    - Arts was severely lacking in network ports and the ports were almost never utilized in the Lecture Hall. There are still 4 live network drops in the front of the Lecture Hall
    - Two more network ports were found to be dead in the new Arts room, and require rewiring in order to function. We are working on getting these working.
    - A new line was run for the camera to be relocated to the new Arts room.
  - Most of the public-use HP laptops are locked up until their configurations can be corrected.
    - There was an unauthorized change in Operating Systems (and its configuration) that caused this.
  - Power is being run for the new Lasersaur, Server Room, 3D Fabrication, and the Woodworking room.
