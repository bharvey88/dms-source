## Basics

**THIS EQUIPMENT REQUIRES TRAINING. DO NOT USE UNLESS YOU HAVE TAKEN THE CLASS AND BEEN SIGNED OFF BY A QUALIFIED INSTRUCTOR!**

This machine is located in the Machine Shop and belongs to the Plastics Special Interest Group (PIG-SIG).

Dewalt DWE7480, Type 1, 10" Table Saw

120V, 15A, 60Hz 4800/min (RPM)

## Rules

1.  **THIS EQUIPMENT REQUIRES TRAINING. DO NOT USE UNLESS YOU HAVE TAKEN THE CLASS AND BEEN SIGNED OFF BY A QUALIFIED INSTRUCTOR!**
2.  Only specific materials are authorized. See the [MATERIALS](/dms-source/archive/machine-shop/shapeoko-3xxl/#Materials) section for details.
3.  All materials currently stored in the SIG area are for committee and class use only. All members must bring their own materials for projects.
4.  Follow all Machine Shop and DMS wide safety rules.

## Materials

ALLOWED:

- Plastics only

NOT ALLOWED:

- Metals
- Any cutting oil or coolant
- Fiberglass or composites
- Wood (allowed only with the explicit, advance permission of the committee chair - and we can't think of a good reason why this would be given)
