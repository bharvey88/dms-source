## Election Time and Place Information

The Dallas Makerspace election will be held at Dallas Makerspace on Friday night, May 10th from 6:30 to 8:30. Polls will close, votes will be counted then the results will be announced. You must be in line by 8:30 for your vote and any proxies to be counted.



## No Electronic Voting

Electronic voting is not being allowed this year because of issues in the Bylaws.



## Importance of Changes to the Bylaws

To get the 2020 elections to run cleanly and smoothly, the Bylaws need to be modified. The Bylaw changes will be listed here as soon as they are received.
