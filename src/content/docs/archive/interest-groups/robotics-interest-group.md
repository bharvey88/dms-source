**Interest Groups are voluntary groups, formed by members in order to find others interested in the topic and achieve certain goals.**\

## Purpose

The Robotics Interest Group is about design, construction, operation, and application of robots. We are people interested in all forms of robots from the small to the large industrial robots.\
We are part of the [Science Committee](/dms-source/archive/science/)

## Meetings

We meet generally at 3pm on Sundays in the Science Area of DMS

## Tools & Equipment

- Two RX-60 Robotic Arms
- [Stäubli CS7MB Robotic Controller](/dms-source/archive/electronics/staubli-robots/)

## How To Join

Add your name to the members section of this wiki. Also, add your talk handle if you wish.
