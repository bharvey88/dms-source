Refunds for membership dues paid within the last 30 days can be authorized according to the limits below:

\- Infrastructure volunteers - up to \$100.00

\- President or Treasurer - \$100.01 to \$1,000.00

\- Both President and Treasurer \$1,000.01 to \$2,000.00

\- Board vote - \$2,000.01 and up.

Refund requests must be made through the WHMCS ticket system from the account that is to be refunded.  Refunds will not be granted based on emails/tickets that do not match the account.

If a refund request occurs due to an error on the part of Dallas Makerspace, the time limit to request a refund may be extended at the discretion of the appropriate volunteer, officer(s) or board members.

Refunds must be credited back to the card on the account.
