## Bridgeport Training Material

Bridgeport training consists of two parts.  The first step is Self-Study.  Once self study is successfully completed, machine training can be scheduled.  Once all training is complete, members are authorized to use either mill.

## Part I - Self-Study

Part I training (self-study) for Bridgeport Mill is required ***prior*** to mandatory machine-side training.

Login to <https://learn.dallasmakerspace.org> and select the Bridgeport Self Study lessons <https://learn.dallasmakerspace.org/course/view.php?id=3> and complete the class with 100% passing score.

[Bridgeport101](/dms-source/machine-shop/tools/mills/vertical-knee-mills/bridgeport-series-1-milling-machine-vertical-knee-mill/early-training-slides-bridgeport-101/) is provided as an historical reference and been supplanted by the above self-study and quiz.

Part II - Machine-Side Training and Check Out\
----------------------------------------------

We watch the submissions from Part I to help gauge demand for Part II. Once we see sufficient demand, we will post a class to the [calendar](https://calendar.dallasmakerspace.org/?tool=34).
