As part of your membership you have a "@dallasmakerspace.org" email address.

To access:

Log into GMAIl.COM in the usual fashion using "YOUR_DMS_USERNAME@dallasmakerspace.org".

You will be directed to a page that looks like this where you will use YOUR_DMS_USERNAME and regular password to login to GMAIL.
