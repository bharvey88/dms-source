In-Progress Shelves:

- Bats must be labeled with **name and date** when the piece was thrown (masking tape and sharpie, 2 labels one on bat and one on bag recommended).
- Pieces on bats/boards that are unlabeled will be **disposed**.
- Pieces will only be allowed to remain on bats for a maximum of **one week** before being moved onto ware boards where they can remain for the remainder of the three weeks (21 days total).
- Labels must be transferred from bats to ware boards.
- Pieces on labeled ware boards will be disposed of **21 days after the labeled *thrown* date**.
- If you are working on a larger piece that requires more than 21 days, tape a note to it or message on talk @Team_Ceramics

Pick-up Shelves:

- Abandoned bisque fired pieces on the pick-up shelves will be disposed of after **21 days**
- Abandoned glaze fired pieces on the pick-up shelves will be disposed of after **21 days**
