When you first setup your account Easel is activated as the Pro version, thus you have access to all of the additional features listed on the ([Easel Standard(Free) vs. Easel Professional(Paid).](/dms-source/woodshop/x-carve-cnc-station/easel-software-getting-started/easel-standard-free-vs-easel-professional-paid/)) page.  After 30 days your account reverts to the free version.

The following steps will aid you in signing up for your Easel software access.

1\. Visit <https://www.inventables.com/pages/easel>

2\. Click on the "Start Free Trial" button.

<img src="/dms-source/files/embedded/WOOD/creating-your-free-easel-account/start-free-trial.png" draggable="false" height="250" />

3\.  Fill out the following form, then click "Start Free Trial" button.

<img src="/dms-source/files/embedded/WOOD/creating-your-free-easel-account/sign-up-form.png" draggable="false" height="400" />

4\. Easel automatically open to the quick start tutorial.

**Note:** You might want to bookmark this page for future access.

You're now ready to begin learning hoe to use the Easel software to design and make great things out of wood.
