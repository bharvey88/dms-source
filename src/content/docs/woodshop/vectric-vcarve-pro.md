This is a premium product offering made available to members of the Makerspace.

## Details

We have the 'Makerspace' edition of this software and you can read more about this offering and it's benefits and limitations at this [Vectric site](https://www.vectric.com/support/makerspace-sign-up).

We have 5 PC's licensed at DMS that can export toolpaths, the maximum allowed.  This full version of the software is called the *Makerspace Edition*.  The software you install on your personal machine is often referred to as either *Makerspace Client Edition or Trial Edition. * In effect, once the *Trial Edition* is installed and tied to a Makerspace ID it becomes the *Makerspace Client Edition*.

## Key points of Makerspace Licensing

The license agreement for the Makerspace Edition only allows linked makerspace users to load files at DMS and export tool paths on our 5 machines for cutting on CNC machines at our makerspace and it is expressly forbidden to generate toolpaths this way for use on CNC machines not present at the Makerspace premises. If these conditions are violated, the VCarve Pro Makerspace license will be revoked.

In order to export tool paths you must use a specific user/pass on one of the 5 machines listed.  (TBD: How to distribute that login info - same issue as above with Makerspace ID really)
