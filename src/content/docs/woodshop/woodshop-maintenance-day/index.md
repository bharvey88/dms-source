This event is held monthly and all members that use the Woodshop are encouraged to attend.  The event is typically held on a Saturday morning and begins at 9:00AM.  Everyone meets in the Annex and Task sheets are handed out.  There is usually a task that suits everyone.  We inventory supplies, organize, and clean, lube, and maintain our equipment.

<p>Time to Clean and Adjust the Equipment<br />
<br />
Everyone is welcome to come out and learn how to keep the shop running.<br />
<br />
Every piece of equipment has a check list and we use the buddy system. After you select a station that you are interest in, you will be paired up with someone who is experienced, they share the tips and secrets for keeping the machine running smoothly. This is a great way to learn how to keep your personal shop running in the future.<br />
<br />
Pick something you are curious about.<br />

- Miter Station<br />
- Band Saws<br />
- Felder<br />
- Sanding machines<br />
- Sanding Station<br />
- Jointer<br />

- Vacuums<br />
- Clean, organize, and inventory items. Make lists for replacement parts and shop supplies.<br />
