The Jointer is used to flatten the surface of a board and its adjacent edge.

- Only solid lumber is allowed. Do not use plywood or other engineered wood (OSB, MDF, ect)
- **Never** surface end-grain on the jointer.
- Verify your lumber does not contain any metal (nails, staples, ect)
- Boards must be 7" or longer and no wider than 12".
