Powermatic Planer 209HH-1

<img src="/dms-source/files/mw/a_a7_Powermatic209HH.png" draggable="false" />



The Planer is used to flatten the surface of a board and its adjacent edge.

- Only solid lumber is allowed. Do not use plywood or other engineered wood (OSB, MDF, ect)
- **Never** surface end-grain on the planer.
- Verify your lumber does not contain any metal (nails, staples, ect)
- Only raise the table a 1/4 to 1/2 turn

Material should be passed through the Jointer first, the flat face will be placed down against the table as it passes through the machine.
