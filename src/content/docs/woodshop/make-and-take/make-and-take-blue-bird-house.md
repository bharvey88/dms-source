Woodshop held its first **Make & Take** class last night. These classes are activity based and will cover different aspects of woodworking. Some will be simple and not require any existing woodworking experience, while others will be a bit more advanced. We hope to develop activities for every skill level.

Our first class lead by none other than [@BirdMan](https://talk.dallasmakerspace.org/u/birdman), made what else but a bird house. Each bird house was made from a precut cedar fence picket. The attendees are just beginning their wood crafting journeys. They experienced using plans, tape measures, hammers, nails, wood glue, screws, drilling a hole, and having lots of fun.

**Make & Take Class:** Build a Blue-Bird House\
**Experience required:** None\
**Tool Authorizations required:** None\
**Time Budget:** 2 Hours
