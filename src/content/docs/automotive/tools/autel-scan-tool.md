The Autel scan tool uses dongles to wirelessly connect to a vehicles OBDII port and provide diagnostic information.

The tool can provide DTCs from a wide variety of modules. It can also provide live data and perform active tests.

After use, please ensure the tablet is connected to the charger, and the dongle is removed from the vehicle.
