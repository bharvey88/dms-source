- Ideal kit for pounding out auto sheet metal
- Comprehensive hammer and dolly repair set features all tools necessary for automobile fender, bumper and large or small body dent repairs
- Hammers and dollies are suited for flattening metal panels, removing dings and dents, creating outside curves and shrinking metal for repairs
- Come with an easy to carry blow-moulded case
