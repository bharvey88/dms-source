The **EnviroQuip A40** is an automated, aqueous (water-based) cleaning system designed to remove heavy grease, oil, and grime from mechanical parts. Unlike traditional solvent sinks, the A40 uses high-temperature water and specialized detergents to "blast" parts clean, making it safer for both the user and the environment.

**<img src="/dms-source/files/embedded/AUTO/parts-washer-turntable-enviroquip-a40-offline/COREL_TURNTABLE_MODELS.jpg" draggable="false" /> **



**How it Works**

- **Rotary Cleaning:** Parts are placed on a motorized turntable that rotates through high-pressure spray jets.

- **Heated Solution:** The system maintains a temperature between **140°F and 195°F** to break down tough contaminants.

- **Integrated Oil Skimming:** An "Oil Wheel" system removes floating oil from the water surface, extending the life of the cleaning solution.

### **Quick Specs for Users**

- **Wash Timer:** Up to 60 minutes.

- **Chemistry:** Uses **EQ3 Cleaner** and **De-Foam concentrate** (Solvents are strictly prohibited).

- **Safety:** Equipped with a door interlock switch that kills the pump if the lid is opened during a cycle.

### **Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)**

1.  **Check Water Level:** Ensure the water is just below the oil skimmer exit pipe before starting.

2.  **Load Parts:** Distribute weight evenly on the turntable. Ensure no parts are small enough to fall through the grate.

3.  **Set Heat:** Turn on the 12-hour heat timer (solution must be hot for effective cleaning).

4.  **Set Wash:** Close the lid securely and set the 60-minute wash timer.

5.  **Post-Wash:** Allow parts to flash-dry from the residual heat before handling.



**\[!CAUTION\] FIRE HAZARD: Never add solvents or pre-treat parts with flammable liquids. Only use approved aqueous detergents. Never perform the "Water Disposal" feature if there is a layer of oil on the water.**
