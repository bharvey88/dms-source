For removal and installation of all types of bearings, bushes and seals. It is designed to remove and install bearings including Silent Bearings, Hydraulic Bearings, Bearing Bushes, Ball Bearings, Shaft Seals, Rubber Bearings and many more. It is crafted to remove bushes, bearing or seals without the need of a press and to reduce the possibility of damaging the bearings/suspension housings.
