If you've purchased a rusty 1990s Honda / Acura, then you may need to replace the perished bushings on the rear trailing arms. This kit allows you to press out the old, and press in the new.



Make sure you oil the tip and the threads, then use a 14mm socket and breaker bar to drive them in / out.
