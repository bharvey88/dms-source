**Paintless Dent Repair Tool Kit**

For sheet metal processing, auto body, and motorcycle dent repair, the Kauplus Adhesive Glue Pulling Hammer Set is designed for efficient and effective dent removal. This comprehensive kit is perfect for fixing car dings, auto dents, and hail damage with minimal expense and maximum convenience. Allegedly

#### Key Features:

**Effortless Operation:** This tool kit is designed for easy use, allowing you to fix car dents, auto dents, and hail damage with minimal effort. The no-prep, no-heating process ensures quick and efficient repairs, saving you time and money.

**Comprehensive Set Contents:**

- **1 x Sliding Hammer (2.8 lb):** Provides the necessary force for effective dent pulling.
- **4 x Aluminum Alloy Adapters:** Includes 2 durable rectangular adapters and 2 round adapters for versatile application.
- **1 x Re-Usable Adhesive Glue (50 g):** Special adhesive designed to pull dents without damaging the paint.
