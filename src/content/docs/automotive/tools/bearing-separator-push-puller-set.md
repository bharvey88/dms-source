This combination set includes two  bearing "splitters" (2" and 3"), four sets of hex push-puller legs and a bar-type puller head with a 9/16" forcing screw. Separator tools are used with bar puller and legs for a wide variety of pulling jobs.\

The 5" puller cross-bar with a 6-1/4" forcing screw or each of the bearing separators may also be used separately or in combination with other pullers or tools.
