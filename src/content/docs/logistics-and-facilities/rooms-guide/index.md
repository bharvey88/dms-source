## Classrooms Guide

There are five general usage "classrooms" available for members to schedule at Dallas Makerspace. Most have some type of electronic projection capability, documented at this link on the **[Classrooms page](/dms-source/logistics-and-facilities/rooms-guide/classrooms/)**. Select the room that is most appropriate for your class.

***Per the fire code, the max capacity for each of our classrooms is 49 persons (no classroom has more than one usable exit).***

Rules regarding classes and external events are listed** [at this link](/dms-source/rules-and-policies/#RulesandPolicies-Classes&Events).**
