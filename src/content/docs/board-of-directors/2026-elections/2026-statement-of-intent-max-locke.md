I am submitting this Statement of Intent to request your consideration for a reappointment to the 2026-2027 DMS Board Of Directors.

Why me?

I believe that a Board of Directors Member needs to:

- have had some business acclimatization - DMS is a \$1M+ per year business, with no employees. I am a Management Consultant by day and have considerable experience with governance, marketing, operations, finance, etc. within a wide range of businesses.
- have had exposure to the inter-workings of DMS' administration. I have been an active participant within numerous DMS Committees, 2024-2025 DMS Secretary, 2025-2026 Board of Directors, and 2025-2026 Vice President of Internal Affairs. These activities and roles have given me considerable exposure.
- focus on what's best for DMS-wide/as a whole, not just a committee, group of members, or oneself.  I think my actions have shown a DMS view.
- be around - an absentee Board of Directors Member does no one any good.  My family jokes that I am at DMS too much.  :)

To conclude, my 2025-2026 goals my myself and hopefully my fellow Board Members include:

- Volunteers
- Teachers, including honorarium adjustments, as needed
- Tour Guides
- Getting Phase II done.  I am so tired of talking about it

If you want to talk about "How?,"  come to the Meet the Candidates Event on Tuesday March 31, 2026 at 7:00 PM in the North Lobby.  I love talking about DMS,



In addition, if you have questions/suggestions/etc. beyond what would be covered in a Meet The Candidates Event, reach out of Talk or Discord, catch me in the halls, catch me in a committee meeting, let's talk about what is on your mind



As always, thanks for the time

## Signature: Max A. Locke
