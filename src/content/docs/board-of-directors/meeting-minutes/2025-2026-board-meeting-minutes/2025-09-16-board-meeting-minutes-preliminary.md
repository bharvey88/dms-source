<td>Board Rule 2526.01<br />
Submission of Proposals Ahead of Board Actions</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-highlight-colour="#deebff"><p><strong>Purpose &amp; Relevance:</strong></p>
<p><strong>aka</strong> <strong>Problem &amp; Solution:</strong></p></td>
<td><p><br />
</p></td>

<tr>
<td data-highlight-colour="#deebff"><strong>Motion Text:</strong></td>
<td><p>Resolution:</p>
<p>Whereas the 2025-2026 DMS Board has more quality proposals that can be addressed<br />
with the DMS current cash position, the 2025-2026 DMS Board chooses to implement a<br />
Board Rule regarding the submission of proposals for consideration of a Board vote to<br />
allow for review, questions, prioritization, etc.</p>
<p>Motion to adopt a Board Rule:</p>
<p><br />
For any request that requires Board approval, whether it is a cash or non-cash request,<br />
a written proposal must be submitted 48-hours ahead of the working session/board mtg<br />
it is to be considered. The proposal should be submitted to<br />
<a href="mailto:admin@dallasmakerspace.org">admin@dallasmakerspace.org</a>.<br />
The proposal should include, at a minimum:<br />
Background<br />
Request - monetary and non-monetary cost<br />
Activation Considerations, if an event<br />
Benefits which might include member satisfaction, operational efficiencies, new member<br />
acquisition, etc.<br />
Risks</p>
<p>Exceptions can be made to this rule by a two-thirds vote of the Board.</p>
<p>This Board Rule does not affect the submission of Emergency Requests. Those<br />
requests will be considered in the Emergency Business section of the Board Agenda,<br />
and the emergency reason will need to be justified.</p>
<p>This Board rule will terminate the last day of service of the 2025-2026 Board.</p>
<p>The Board instructs the Secretary to develop a new section in the rules for placing these<br />
Board Rules.</p>
<p>The Board instructs the Vice President of Internal Affairs to communicate this Board<br />
Rule to all Committee Chairs.</p></td>
</tr>
