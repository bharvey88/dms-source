<td data-cf-field="title">Extending Honorarium to Tour Guides</td>
<td data-cf-field="problem">Tour Guide Shortage</td>
<td data-cf-field="Solution"><p>Whereas: </p>
<p>Tour Guides are a key part of DMS’ new member acquisition strategy</p>
<p>We are currently Tour Guide limited on both Thursday nights and Saturday morning</p>
<p>DMS has used its communication tools in order solicit new Tour Guides</p>
<p>Lack of Tour Guides limits our social media activation because of demand concerns</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Motion to add a new section to the Honorarium section of the DMS Rules</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In furtherance of our mission, Dallas Makerspace may pay honorarium to tour guides as a “thank you” for giving tours.  The rules below govern the tour guide honorarium program and may be modified by the Board of Directors at any time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Honorarium Payment Policies – Tour Guides</p>
<p>1.       Honorarium payments to tour guides will be $_______ per “tour session.”</p>
<p>2.       Tour Guides will need to participate in a Tour Guide training session to be eligible.</p>
<p>3.       Tours Sessions will be limited to _______ Tour Guides receiving honorarium.</p>
<p>4.       For all Tour Guide honorariums, the Tour Guide must participate for a minimum of two hours during the Tour Session.</p>
<p>a.    Activities include: For potential members, actively greeting at the door, helping with waivers, understanding interests, giving the tours, helping with sign-ups.</p>
<p>b.    Being on-call (present at the space but not actively touring) will not qualify for a honorarium.</p>
<p>5. Honorarium payments will be limited to six (6) payments per member, per month.  This limit includes both teaching and Tour Guide activities. This limit will be periodically reviewed and adjusted by the Board of Directors.</p>
<p>6.    Tour Guides will have the option to donate the honorariums they are eligible for to the committee of their choice.</p></td>
