<td data-cf-field="createdBy">Ian Lee</td>
<td data-cf-field="title">Evaluate Manufacturing of Carbon Fiber</td>
<td data-cf-field="problem">Working on Carbon fiber parts is currently not allowed due to unwarranted historic concerns.</td>
<td data-cf-field="Solution">Working on cured Carbon fiber parts is currently not allowed. Discussion years ago that led to this ban focused on the danger of Carbon Fiber dust. <br />
Carbon fiber dust does, in fact, pose a health risk. But so does most of our other dust making processes at DMS. For example:
<ul>
<li>Sanding and lathe Dust from exotic woods in the woodshop can cause severe allergic reactions, and if inhaled can cause lung issues. </li>
<li>Working on exotic woods in general can aerosolize oils that can pose lung issues. </li>
<li>Grinding in metal shop can produce micro metallic dust which can be inhaled </li>
<li>Silica from dried  pottery clay can be inhaled and directly cause silicosis</li>
<li>Overheated cutting fluid from machine shop aerosolizes and can be irritating to lungs </li>
<li>Any of the various chemicals that are used for cleaning, and general work, from resins, to finishing products, to brake cleaner, and so on all produce inhalation hazards. </li>
<li>And so on </li>
</ul>
<br />
Simply put, any maker should be cognizant that the processes to make things poses dangers, dust poses dangers, chemicals pose dangers, and caution when working on parts should always be taken. Likewise, working at the space where these processes are underway must be accepted  and understood in general. <br />
The same general rules must, however, apply: cleaning after oneself to reduce the risk to others is paramount for carbon fiber, just as it is for clay, grinding, or woodwork. <br />
I’m encouraging the board to reconsider this unfounded ban, and instead encourage making cool stuff, use of self provided proper appropriate PPE for all work, and cleaning after oneself. <br />
<strong>Tabled - Jack to create and lead group to research safety standards and present an agenda item and recommendations to the board.</strong></td>
<td data-cf-field="Relevance">Let’s make cool stuff and not ban a material or process due to unfounded fears.</td>
