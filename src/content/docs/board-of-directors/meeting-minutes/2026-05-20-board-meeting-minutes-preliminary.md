<td data-cf-field="Solution"><p>Board Rule 2627.01</p>
<p>Submission of Proposals Ahead of Board Actions</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Resolution</p>
<p>Whereas the 2026-2027 DMS Board has more quality proposals that can be addressed with the DMS current cash position, the 2026-2027 DMS Board chooses to implement a Board Rule regarding the submission of proposals for consideration of a Board vote to allow for review, questions, prioritization, etc.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Motion to adopt a Board Rule</p>
<p>For any request that requires Board approval, whether it is a cash or non-cash request, a written proposal must be submitted two weeks ahead of the board mtg it is to be considered.  This two-week window will allow the proposal to be discussed at the work session two weeks prior to consideration.  The proposal should be submitted to admin@dallasmakerspace.org.</p>
<p>The proposal should include, at a minimum:</p>
<p>Background</p>
<p>Request - monetary and non-monetary</p>
<p>Cost</p>
<p>Activition Considerations, if an event</p>
<p>Benefits which might include member satisfaction, operational efficiencies, new member acquisition, etc.</p>
<p>Risks</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Exceptions can be made to this rule by a two-thirds vote of the Board.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>This Board Rule does not affect the submission of Emergency Requests.  Those requests will be considered in the Emergency Business section of the Board Agenda, and the emergency reason will need to be justified.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>This Board rule will terminate the last day of service of the 2026-2027 Board.</p>
<p> </p>
<p> </p>
<p>The Board instructs the Secretary to develop a new section in the rules for placing these Board Rules</p>
<p>The Board instructs the Vice President of Internal Affairs to communicate this Board Rule to all Committee Chairs.</p></td>

<td data-cf-field="title">Expand Honorarium to Tour Guides</td>
<td data-cf-field="problem">Need to increase incentives to get more consistent tour guide turnout</td>
<td data-cf-field="Solution"><p>Background:</p>
<p>By providing the Tour Guides an honorarium we project that we can increase the number of tour guides, the quality of tour guides and thereby increase our close ratios, increasing revenue.</p>
<p>Cost: ~ $2.2K/mth</p>
<p>5 tour guides per tour session x 2 session per week x 4.33 weeks per month X $50/ session</p>
<p>Revenue: ~ $8.5 to $17K / mth</p>
<p>increase close ratio by 10% to 20% by 1) training to increase the knowledge and skill of the tour guides 2) increasing tour guide count will decrease tour group sizes, increasing effectiveness</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Motion to expand honorarium to tour guides by replacing the current "Honorarium Rules" section in the rules, with the following:</p>
<p><strong>Honorarium Rules</strong></p>
<p>In furtherance of our mission, Dallas Makerspace may provide an honorarium to teachers or other volunteers in specific instances, as a “thank you.”.  The rules below govern the honorarium program and may be modified by the Board of Directors at any time.</p>
<p><strong>Requirements/Limitations</strong></p>
<ol type="1">
<li>All persons wishing to collect honorarium in excess of $600 per year must provide a W-9 to DMS’ bookkeepers.</li>
<li>Honorarium payments will be limited to eight (8) per member, per month.  This limitation will be periodically reviewed and adjusted by the Board of Directors.</li>
<li>For the duration of their term, no Director may accept honorariums directly from the Dallas Makerspace. </li>
</ol>
<p> </p>
<p>Teaching Specific Requirements:</p>
<ol start="3" type="1">
<li><ol type="1">
<li>All classes are subject to scrutiny especially where subject matter is vague, potentially unrelated to making, or instruction time is less than 90 minutes to ensure quality of material and value to the DMS and members.</li>
<li>Honorarium auditors cannot approve their own classes or events where honorarium has been requested.</li>
</ol></li>
</ol>
<p>Teacher Honorarium Payment Policies</p>
<p>1.       Honorarium payments to teachers will be $50.00 per class taught provided subject and attendance requirements are met.</p>
<p>2.       Teachers will have the option to donate the honorariums they are eligible for to the committee of their choice at the time the class is posted on the DMS calendar.</p>
<p>3.       For all honorarium classes, 3 attendees are required to receive honorarium.</p>
<p>a.       Upon request by the Committee Chair, the Board may approve an exception for this class attendance minimum if the 1) class size is limited because of access to tools, 2) focuses on safety in nature, 3) is required for tool access, AND 4) is in high demand. This exception does not require the Chair to fully or even partially implement the exception.</p>
<p>4.       When a honorarium is requested by a teacher, any class fees requested by the teacher can not exceed the cost of materials and supplies. ( <a href="/dms-source/board-of-directors/meeting-minutes/2024-2025-board-meeting-minutes/2024-07-10-board-meeting-minutes-approved/">BoD Reference</a> )</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Tour Guide Specific Requirements:</p>
<p>1.       After a proper transition period, tour guide training may be required to obtain an honorarium.</p>
<p>Tour Guide Honorarium Payment Policies:</p>
<p>1.       Honorarium payments will be $50.00 per tour guide shift (entire tour period on any given day).</p>
<p>2.       For all Tour Guide honorariums, a maximum of five (5) Tour Guides may claim an honorarium for each “shift.”</p>
<p>3.       Tour Guides will have the option to donate the honorariums they are eligible for to the committee of their choice.</p>
<p>4.       Specific Tour Guide Honorarium procedures will be developed by the V.P. of Marketing and the Treasurer, or their designee.</p>
