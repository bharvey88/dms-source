My name is Julie Harris, Julie-Harris on Talk, and this is formal notice of my intent to run for re-election to the Dallas Makerspace Board of Directors. I have been a member of Dallas Makerspace since 2016 and I have served on the board since 2019.  Other positions held at Dallas Makerspace include President, Secretary, PR Coordinator, Education Coordinator and Digital Media chair.

I would like to continue to volunteer my time to serve on the board of Dallas Makerspace because I believe in the mission of education and collaboration.  I also believe that knowledge and opportunity should be available to everyone.  Serving on the board has been one of my most rewarding experiences and I would be honored to have the opportunity to serve for another term.  If I am not elected, I will continue to volunteer and contribute to DMS.

My background is in accounting and I currently work as a fractional controller for several clients.  When I’m not volunteering for Dallas Makerspace, I am working on my own projects at DMS, hiking and spending time with friends and family.

**Director & Officer accomplishments include:**

- Lease renewal with TI allowance - negotiated to add Automotive to the renewal
- Effectively responded to challenges presented by COVID-19
- Lobby remodel
- Established a management structure to separate the board from day to day operations and increase accountability
- Recovered from negative cash flow and improved cash management
- Increased professionalism and efficiency of board meetings

**Goals for the next term:**

- Take advantage of the tenant improvement allowance to improve the appearance of Dallas Makerspace
- Support efforts to create online content for both PR and education
- Continue work to keep DMS a positive place for people to learn and make
- Continue to recruit new members
- Support PR and fundraising efforts

Signed: Julie Harris
