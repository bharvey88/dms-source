Hello, I am Jayson Woods, and I am running for election to the DMS Board of Directors for 2023. My primary intent is to ensure that the organization continues to thrive as a vibrant and inclusive community of makers, fostering creativity, education, and innovation.

I'm committed to providing members with the resources and support needed to pursue their projects and interests, while also fostering a culture of safety, respect, and collaboration. I will strive to maintain a welcoming and accessible environment for individuals of all backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels.

Furthermore, I aim to strengthen our partnerships with local organizations, businesses, and educational institutions to further promote the growth of the maker community in Dallas and beyond. We will work to expand our outreach and programming to engage with more people and provide meaningful learning experiences.

In all our endeavors, we will prioritize transparency, accountability, and effective communication with our members and stakeholders, seeking feedback and input to inform our decisions and actions. My goal is to continue to be a driving force for creativity, learning, and innovation in the Dallas community and beyond.

**Who am I?**

I’m Jayson Woods … My DMS membership started in 2013 and because I have been around for so long it has giving me a  good understanding of the history of DMS

For work I service and maintain Nurse call systems in hospitals. Previous to that my employment  was with Texas Scottish Rite Hospital. I’ve been in the healthcare field  for 10 years.

Outside of work I spend my time working with other organizations and have been a committee chair at DMS since 2015 in various committees .  I helped keep DMS running during the pandemic by giving constant updates from the CDC, local hospitals, and other government agencies. I Helped DMS 3D print face shields for local  hospitals and clinics. I just finished my term as President of North Texas Corviar Association (3 year term due to Covid). I'm currently the webmaster for Classic Chassis car club.

**Questions?**

You can message me on Talk (username engpin) or on the DMS Discord.

If I am elected, I'll do everything I can to make this the best makerspace in DFW.

If I am not elected, I will still be contributing to the space, volunteering.
