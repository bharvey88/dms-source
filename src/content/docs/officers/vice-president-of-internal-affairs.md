**Vice President of Internal Affairs**

Responsible for the overall leadership and direction of DMS’ internal functions, including:

- Education
- Volunteers
- Members/Community
- Committees
- DMS-Wide Projects

The role will also include the appropriate spending limits for the above mentioned activities, as deemed by the Treasurer and documented in the DMS rules.
