**Vice President of External Affairs**

Responsible for the overall leadership and direction of DMS’ external functions, including:

- Sponsorships/Partnerships/Donations
- Outreach – educational, service organizations, etc.
- Grant Acquisition
- Communications resulting from inbound questions/requests regarding DMS (via email, social, etc.)

The role will also include the appropriate spending limits for the above mentioned activities, as deemed by the Treasurer and documented in the DMS rules.
