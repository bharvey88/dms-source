**Vice President of Marketing**

Responsible for the overall leadership and direction of DMS’ marketing activities, including:

- General Marketing
- New Member Acquisition - Direct
- On-Line - Social/Digital
- Public Relations/Press Releases
- Event Marketing (Internal)
- Event Marketing (External)

The role will also include the appropriate spending limits for the above mentioned activities, as deemed by the Treasurer and documented in the DMS rules.
