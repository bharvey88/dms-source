<td>Paul / Michael</td>

<p>We may be able to hack the comms from the ECU to the TCU through a CANBUS hack. If this doesn't work, we could use a WRX manual ECU / TCU</p>
<p>The transmission won't fit in the tunnel - starter on opposite side - we can enlarge the tunnel</p>
<p>We may need to build a transmission support - we feel comfortable with this</p>

<td>Alternatives </td>
<td>All</td>
<td><p>could we remove the WRX interior and cage it? This was make the car less accessible to new members - having 5 seats allows for better transfer of driving skills</p>
<p>Could we put in a BRZ engine and transmission and sell the WRX engine? We don't think that saves us much, and loses power</p>
<p>Could we sell the WRX shell and get a different second car? We don't think we'd get a low milage, 5 seater car that would be useful for track and rallycross for the price of the WRX</p></td>
