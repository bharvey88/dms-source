Best towed with a Weight Distributing Hitch

**Registration **

My advice is just don't let the registration expire. If it does expire, you'll need to do the following:



1.  Make sure there are no unpaid tickets outstanding. You'll need to swear that there aren't later
2.  Print out the form below to register the trailer
3.  Make yourself a DallasMakerspace business card. This is questionable, but you know, it's what they need to see. Poke around on the PR committee folders and find a template and just add your name to one. Print it out
4.  Spend hours waiting at the Dallas County tax office. Question your life choices. Take a book.
5.  Pay them some money - discount for cash. Debit and credit card have a roughly three dollar fee as of 2025
