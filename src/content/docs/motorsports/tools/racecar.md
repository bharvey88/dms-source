## Fire Bottle:

Lifeline Zero 2000 AFFF - foam based. Needs to meet " SFI 17.1- or FIA 8865 Technical List 16- or Technical List 52-certified onboard fire-suppression system is mandatory" per [Lemons](https://24hoursoflemons.com/prices-rules/#safety)

Needs to be serviced every two years (October 2025)

Lifeline themselves service it, as does their certified dealer, Chris Taylor in Austin. Posting it: there's apparently nothing illegal about posting it via UPS, just label it  *auto parts* to avoid confusion
