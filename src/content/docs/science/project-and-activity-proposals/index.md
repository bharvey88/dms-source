Please post your project ideas and proposals here as a separate page with a bit of background and a description of what you want to do and why you think it would be a good group project. The type of project that is preferred either involves new research that advances the state of open-source knowledge, synergizes with the needs of other communities here at DMS, or is related to making tools and equipment that enhance our ability to do more science.

Fun Activities for Science Sunday can also be proposed here.
