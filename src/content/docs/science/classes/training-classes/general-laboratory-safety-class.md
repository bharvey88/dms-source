The General and Chemical Laboratory Safety class will cover basic safety procedures around handling chemicals, disposing of waste, pressurized gas cylinders, and other topics.  This class will equip you to be able to read an SDS and understand the hazards that chemicals may present and the appropriate PPE and procedures to minimize risk.

**General Laboratory Safety is a PREREQUISTE for almost every other science class being developed.**
