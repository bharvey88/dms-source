The following are the classes that have been developed at the Science Committee.  In general they consist of three types:

1.  **Trainings**
2.  **Demonstrations and Learning**
3.  **Hands-on Classes/Make-and-Take**

Typically, training classes will enable attendees to either gain permission or further develop skills necessary to use equipment here in the Science area.  Demonstrations and learning classes are more geared towards larger groups and for safety or other reasons do not have a hands-on component.  Our hands-on-classes either offer opportunity to try an experimental procedure for yourself or to make something fun that you can take with you.
