Procedure:

1.  Prepare a 2% w/v solution of Sodium Tetraborate (Borax)
2.  Purchase poly-vinyl alcohol based school glue
3.  Dilute school glue with an equal amount of water (preferably warm)
4.  (Optional) Add food coloring as desired to glue solution
5.  Add 2% Borax solution while stirring slowly until desired consistency is obtained.
