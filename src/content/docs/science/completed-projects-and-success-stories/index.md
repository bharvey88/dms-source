### ***“Freely share its research and discoveries***, using what is learned to teach others.” - DMS Mission Statement 4

This set of completed projects pages is intended to serve as a repository for the results, findings, and discoveries generated as a result of committee group projects. We intend that group projects be open source as much as possible. We intend that project descriptions, photographs, build instructions, collected data, source code, 3D files, and other information be freely available here. The Science Committee aspires to generate new and useful knowledge that promotes scientific, cultural, and artistic advancement.

Here we also celebrate the accomplishments of our members as it relates to personal projects and endeavors related to work done at DMS Science.
