The Science area at the Dallas Makerspace has a fume hood and an assortment of chemical glassware to support chemistry research and educational outreach. Current classes include soap making and silver mirroring but many more are under development. A laboratory lab safety and basic lab procedures class is REQUIRED to use the fume hood.
