**Supersaturated Sodium Acetate**

<img src="https://source.dallasmakerspace.org/download/attachments/embedded-page/SCIENCE/Science%20Sunday%20%239%20-%20Supersaturated%20Sodium%20Acetate%20(DIY%20Hand%20Warmer)%20+%20Radiative%20Cooling%20Fabric/20250222_141206.jpg" draggable="false" height="250" />

Acetic acid (vinegar) was neutralized with sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) and the resulting sodium acetate solution heated to concentrate the solution.

<img src="https://source.dallasmakerspace.org/download/attachments/embedded-page/SCIENCE/Science%20Sunday%20%239%20-%20Supersaturated%20Sodium%20Acetate%20(DIY%20Hand%20Warmer)%20+%20Radiative%20Cooling%20Fabric/image-2025-3-14_10-24-29.png" draggable="false" height="250" />

After cooling, a supersaturated solution was obtained.  Introduction of seed crystals or another source of nucleation sites causes the supersaturated solution to convert to a solid.

<img src="https://source.dallasmakerspace.org/download/attachments/embedded-page/SCIENCE/Science%20Sunday%20%239%20-%20Supersaturated%20Sodium%20Acetate%20(DIY%20Hand%20Warmer)%20+%20Radiative%20Cooling%20Fabric/20250223_154023.jpg" draggable="false" height="250" /><img src="https://source.dallasmakerspace.org/download/attachments/embedded-page/SCIENCE/Science%20Sunday%20%239%20-%20Supersaturated%20Sodium%20Acetate%20(DIY%20Hand%20Warmer)%20+%20Radiative%20Cooling%20Fabric/20250223_164944.jpg" draggable="false" height="250" />

DIY hand warmers were fabricated but the metal click disk initiators made from a tape measure were not very reliable (some initiated without being clicked and others failed to initiate even when clicked).
