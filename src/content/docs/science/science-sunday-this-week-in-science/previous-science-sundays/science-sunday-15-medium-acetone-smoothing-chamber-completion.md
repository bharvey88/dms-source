During the 4-27-25 Science Sunday we completed our medium-sized basic 3D print smoothing chamber with good results. This should be sufficient for a large percentage of use cases although we would like to make an even more ambitious chamber that has finer controls.

Those interested in using the chamber will need to take a class on the operation of the chamber as well as the general laboratory safety class.
