### **Research Project: **3D-Print Vapor Smoothing Chamber Working Session 2

- Two vapor chamber smoothing designs were selected for development.  We will be developing a simple chamber that anyone can build in order to further open source knowledge as well as a more complicated chamber that gives us more control over the process conditions in terms of controlling temperature, pressure, acetone concentrations, etc.
- There will be two main objectives and teams: Build/Design and Metrology. Everyone will be working together during the group working session on Sundays.  Teams are more for volunteering for specific additional take-home work.

**Build Goals for the week:**

- Come up with a initial design and complete BOM so that any necessary parts can be ordered over the Christmas break prior to the next 3D Print Science Sunday in Jan.
- Use CAD software to make feet for the polycarbonate blast shield and 3D print those feet.  (Opportunity for someone interested in learning CAD software to watch/give it a try for a simple object)

**Metrology Goals for the week:**

- Design sample holder and test coupons
- Complete a smoothing demonstration showing the difference between chemical-mechanical pre-polished surfaces and untreated surfaces at various smoothing times
- Get an image of the interface - before and after smoothing to enable development of analysis procedures/software
