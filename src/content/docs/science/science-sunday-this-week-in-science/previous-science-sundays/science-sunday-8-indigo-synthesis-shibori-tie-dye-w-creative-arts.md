**Indigo Synthesis (02/09/25) + Shibori Tie Dye (02/16/25)**

A class introducing the chemistry of dyes and pigments was presented.  Indigo was then synthesized from using the Baeyer-Drewson method from 2-nitrobenzaldehyde and acetone. Then a Creative Arts committee class on Shibori Tie Dye was taught using the dye we made.
