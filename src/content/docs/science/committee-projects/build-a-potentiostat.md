**Objective: **Build 1-2 low cost potentiostats (~\$20-50 each) to support all types of electrochemistry classes.

**Example Classes Enabled by Resource:** Optimization of can-battery, fabrication of dye sensitized solacell (DSSC), electrochromic devices, sensors (various types), water electrolysis, electrocatalysis, metal plating, and other classes.
