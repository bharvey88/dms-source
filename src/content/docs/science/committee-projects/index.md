At our monthly meetings, Science will vote on projects that we work on together as a group during each Science Sunday.  Active and completed projects are listed in this category. Personal projects that are actively being developed at the space can also be listed here but should be labelled with a (Personal) tag.

If you have an idea for a project, please do post it under the projects proposals page so we can consider your idea as we select group projects during our monthly formal meetings.
