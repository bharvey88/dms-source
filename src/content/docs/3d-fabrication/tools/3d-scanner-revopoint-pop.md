## Scanner Rules

1.  Beware the Class 1 IR laser.  Don't shine directly into eyes.  Instruct face-scanning subjects to close eyes during scanning.
2.  Don't drop the scanner!
3.  Don't cross-thread the 1/4" tripod mount.  Always turn the screw in reverse until the threads engage.
4.  Be careful when inserting the delicate USB connectors.  Don't cross-thread the USB connector lugs - always turn the screw in reverse until the threads engage.
5.  When finished, pack the scanner neatly into its protective orange case.
6.  Protect the scanner and turntable from powders, foods, residues, etc.
7.  Training is NOT required to use the scanner.  Online, self-service training is in-development.  Please contact Evan Lott if you'd like to help write the Moodle course.
8.  Immediate report any damage, theft, missing pieces, or other issues to the 3D Fab chairperson, a board member, or an officer.
