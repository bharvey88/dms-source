Notes on usage

- Creality Slicer is currently only on a single PC, closest to the printer
- When removing any print, use the menu controls to propel the belt, and wait until parts are off of the belt before collecting.
- DO NOT PULL ANY PART DIRECTLY OFF OF THE BELT
