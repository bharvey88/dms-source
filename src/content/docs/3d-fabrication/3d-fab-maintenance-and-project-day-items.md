- ## Maintenance



- - ## General

    - Floors Vacuumed
    - Desks wiped / cleaned
    - Clean off computer mouse and keyboards
    - Complete any software upgrades on PCs
    - Purgatory area reviewed for overfilling./ updating.
    - Check Drawers for tools



- - ## Polly Printers

    - Interior vacuumed
    - Bed tape replaced (as needed)
    - preventative maintenance as needed
    - Lemon Pledge Enclosures



- - ## Bambu Printers

    - Interior Vacuumed
    - Wash plates
    - Oil / lube Z-axis Lead Screws
    - Clean off X-axis Carbon Rods
    - Clean off Micro Lidar lens
    - Clean of time-lapse camera
    - Replace Cutters Every 4 months.  Next: November 2025.
    - Other preventative maintenance. (check maintenance screen for messages)
      - Use the link for X1 reference:  <https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/x1/maintenance/basic-maintenance>



- - ## Belt Printer

    - Vacuumed
    - Clean belt
    - Lube / Other preventative maintenance



- - ## Resin Printers

    - Clean printers as needed. (Keith supervise)
