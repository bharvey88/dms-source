Print areas are the same for the MPX-80 and MPX-90. The MPX-90 outperforms the MPX-80 in every other aspect.

Both have a material hardness limit. It must be a Vickers Harness (HV) of 200 or less on the printing surface.

Common Acceptable Material Types:

- Aluminum, Brass, Bronze, Copper, Mild Steel, Yellow Gold, Silver, Nickel (surface must have a luster finish or plated)
- Plastic or acrylic (transparent or semi- transparent for best results

Not Recommended Material Types:

- Material with a Matte surface
- Deep acrylic colors, opaque plastics
- Glass, stone, china, porcelain and wood
- Rubber or elastic type of plastic

Example Curved Surfaces:

- Pen
- Bracelet
- Rings
