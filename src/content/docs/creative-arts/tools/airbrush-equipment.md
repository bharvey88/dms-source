Creative Arts has a variety of airbrush tools **(water-based paints only)** including:

- Airbrushes:
  - Master G22
  - Timbertek AG-183K (a.k.a. Fengda BD-183K)
- Air Compressors:
  - Elite-175X
  - TC-20T
  - Central Pneumatic 93657
- Miscellaneous accessories
