**Button Making Consumables** (\$1 per qty):

- \(8\) 1" buttons,
- \(6\) 1.5" buttons,
- \(4\) 2.25" buttons,
- \(2\) 2.25" Magnetic buttons

Button Making Equipment

- Button Makers (1", 1.5", and 2.25")
- Circle Punches (1", 1.5", and 2.25")
- Adjustable Circle Cutter (up to 3.5")
- Miscellaneous accessories
