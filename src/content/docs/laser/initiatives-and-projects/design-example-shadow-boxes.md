The committee should showcase examples of what the Laser is used for.  Many visitors, and some members really haven't a clue regarding the machines.

The concept is to have standardized Laser cut boxes with an acrylic cover, possibly backlit that hold example projects.  The wall is for goth inspiring and educating people on how long it takes to cut a project.  Each project should be named, along with a link back to the Laser project file area in source.
