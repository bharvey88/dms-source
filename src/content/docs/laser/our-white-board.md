We have a large white board mounted on the wall by the back door.  This board serves a dual purpose.

## Top half of board

Use the top half to make  @Laser_Team aware of Squawks.  These are items that are a nuisance and might be a developing problem.  However, they are not currently serious enough to be reported as a formal issue.



Bottom half of board

Please write any ideas or suggestions regarding how to make the Laser Committee a better place.



There are two columns in each section.

|  |  |
|----|----|
| @\_handle  | We might want to contact you for more details, so please leave us your talk handle. |
| Details | Share your thoughts or ideas. Feel free to use more than one line. |
