### **Laser Memory is Full**

The laser memory full of jobs (Max 99 files) prevents downloading new files. The computer will still say successful.

**How to clear the memory:**

On the Laser:\
Press File\
Right arrow once to the list of menu option\
Down arrow to “other”\
Press Enter\
Down Arrow to “Delete All Files”\
Press Enter to confirm\
Press Esc\
Press Esc
