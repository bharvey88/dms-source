The Sewing Special Interest group is for DMS members who are interested in sewing in a wide variety of projects. Apparel, Quilting, Cosplay, and Fashion- the Sewing Studio has a sewing machine that can work with almost any project. In addition to the equipment owned by DMS, members are encouraged to bring their own sewing machines/sergers to use in classes or at events to further sewing skills.

The Sewing SIG is currently housed under the Creative Arts committee.
