### Bobbins:

<u>***DO NOT BRING YOUR OWN.\***
</u>‘Universal’ bobbins are being sold as class 15 bobbins. It is damaging machines.\
We have many for sale at cost (\$1 for 2 bobbins)

### Needles:

Use what you need. Needles are in the boxes next to the machines.\
If you break more than 1 needle per visit, you need to assess the needle type, materials, or sewing technique.

### Thread Types:

Thread that is out is available for use, however it is donated. Sewing does not buy/stock thread. If you need a particular type or color of thread for your project, you must bring it.

1.  Babylock (spools)
2.  Pfaff/BCMG (will be by the BCMG machine, not there yet)
3.  Serger (cones)
