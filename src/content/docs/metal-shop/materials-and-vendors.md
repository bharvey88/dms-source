## Materials

With the following exceptions, members are expected to provide their own materials and consumables (including angle grinder wheels):

- **MIG Wire.** The fee for using the DMS-provided wire (0.035") in the MIG welders is \$1 for every 3 minutes of continuous welding.
- **Welding Gas.** The gas provided for the welders has no additional cost for members.
- **Powder Coating Powder.** Members can use the powders in the cabinet at no additional cost.



The following vendor lists are not intended to be comprehensive. **If you have a favorite vendor that isn't on the list (or have edits/corrections), feel free to add it.**
