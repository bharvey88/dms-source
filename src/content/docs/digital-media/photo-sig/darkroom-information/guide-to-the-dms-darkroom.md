Access & Orientation
--------------------

**The DMS Darkroom is a specialized space requiring proper training and authorization for use.**

- **Shared Community Space: **This darkroom is a shared community space. It is also used for exposing screens used in screen printing. This page is only relevant for use as an analogue photography darkroom.

- **Mandatory Sign-Off Class:** To gain access to and use the darkroom, all members *must* attend a mandatory orientation and sign-off class. This ensures safe and proper operation of equipment and handling of chemicals.

- **Check the DMS Calendar:** Keep an eye on the DMS Calendar for upcoming darkroom orientation class schedules.

- **Questions?** Reach out to the DMS Photography SIG on Talk or Discord for more information.

## Equipment

The DMS Darkroom is equipped with the following for your black and white printing needs:

- **Enlargers:**

  - **Beseler 23Cii:** A versatile medium format enlarger.

  - **MX45:** A more robust enlarger, suitable for larger format negatives.

  - **Tools: **Set of contrast filters, grain focusing tools, and easels for each booth.

- **Tanks:** Plastic and metal developing tanks and reels for 135,120,110 format film

- **Trays:** Sets of trays for 4x5, 8x10, 11x16, and 16x20 prints

- **Chemistry:** Stock a standard set for film and paper development. See Consumables section for details

## Consumables

**Chemistry and photographic paper are available for purchase at the Square Terminals, or Paypal QR Codes.**

- **Pricing:** Check pricing sheet posted on the wall.

- **Chemistry:** Kodak XTOL\[film\] and LegacyPro Dev\[paper\], Ilfo-stop bath, Ilford rapid fixer, Kodak Hypo-Clearing Agent, and Kodak Photo-flo are stocked

- **Paper:** Pearl Multigrade RC

- **Bring Your Own:** Members are also welcome to bring their own chemistry and paper, provided it is compatible with black and white photographic processes and disposed of properly.

## Rules & Etiquette

To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all users, please adhere to the following:

- **Cleanliness:** Always clean up your workspace thoroughly after each use. This includes wiping down surfaces, rinsing trays, and properly disposing of chemicals.

  - Before starting make sure to put away any dry tools from the sink before you start.

- **Chemical Disposal:** Follow all posted guidelines for chemical disposal. Never pour chemicals down the drain unless explicitly stated.

  - **DMS Chems: ** Fixer **MUST** be poured into the fixer bucket. All other may be poured down the drain
  - **BYO Chems: **You **MUST **take all your spent chemistry back with you. **No BYO chemistry may be poured down the drain **

- **Ventilation:** Ensure the ventilation system is operational when working with chemistry.

- **Safe Light:** Strict darkroom protocols. As a shared space always knock when entering when the 'In use' sign is on. If exiting always ensure all light sensitive materials are covered.

- **Report Issues:** Immediately report any equipment malfunctions, chemical spills, or concerns on Talk @\[Talk group handle TBD\].

- **No Food or Drink:** For safety and to prevent contamination, food/drink is not allowed while working in the darkroom.
