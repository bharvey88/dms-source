## Purpose

The Digital Media Committee's responsibilities are to:

- Maintain the order, organization, safety, cleanliness, and utility of the digital media room and equipment.
- Areas of interest include Audio, Photography, Video, Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality and 3D modeling.
- Provides the means for you to create your own content.
